END-LOFT MASTER RULES TO CARRY FORWARD

1. The trunk and split endings are one physical ribbon/connection structure.
   Apply the same master geometry rules through the fitted cap, reverse-loft
   branch and connection termination. Endings are not exempt transition zones.
   Preserve conductor identities, ordering, prescribed terminal locations and
   orientations. Never relocate targets or add a fallback to obtain a build.

2. Reverse lofting is required for splitting behavior. This is the sole
   construction-method exception; it grants no exception to curvature, length,
   spacing, clearance, sphere containment or smooth approach requirements.

3. Author the cap and branch arrival against the same exact geometric tangent
   and cap plane. A separately sampled legacy frame tangent is not authoritative
   when it differs from the actual cap normal. Mere cap coincidence is insufficient:
   inspect flow and curvature on both sides of the junction and at the connection.
   Matching endpoint tangents alone does not certify the native loft between them.

4. Each end region has a maximum allowance of 6% of the COMPLETE conductor path,
   including the ribbon and both endings. It is not 6% of the end loft itself,
   not a required end length, and not a license to impose long fixed-size endings.
   Where an operation requires a positive dimension, a small positive value must
   still obey the rules; do not clamp an infeasible geometric solution into a pass.
   The older checkpoint may retain a trunk-only conservative authoring allowance:
   distinguish that historical implementation from this retained requirement.

5. Preserve the current 1% length limits for complete conductor paths, trunk
   lanes and paired native ribbon sides, and the existing spacing/pitch policy
   (experimental maximum pitch ratio 1.03). Whole-lane sampled bend radius was
   checked against 3 conductor diameters. The conservative continuous macaroni
   contract bounds curvature times enclosing profile reach at 0.8, including
   branch cross-sections. Sampled checks are not continuous proofs.

6. Current experiment boundary: sphere DIAMETER is 3 times nominal cable width.
   Both full cable-end guides and the occupied structure must remain inside.
   Wrinkles may use available space only within the master rules. Seek shortest
   feasible deviation with fixed targets, not arbitrary loops/excursions to pass.
   Older baseline fixtures use 4x/5x spheres and sometimes unconstrained interiors;
   do not silently describe their historical outcomes as satisfying today's rules.

7. Freeze and validate complete branch plans before native construction; build
   those same plans, rather than calculating endings differently during lofting.
   Experimental diagnostic builds may attempt rejected candidates, but record
   mathematical rejection and actual native outcome separately. No local fixes,
   tolerance weakening, missing-audit success, or fallback geometry.
