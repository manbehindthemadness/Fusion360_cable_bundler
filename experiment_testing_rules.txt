2. Independent configurations are the reporting unit
----------------------------------------------------
Define one case by its complete boundary conditions and required connection layout.
Record conductor count/dimensions, endpoint positions/orientations, split targets,
routing controls/obstacles and constraints. Track parent case IDs for variants.
Different rolls, wrinkle parameters, random solver seeds, repeated builds, section
counts, branch lofts and audit stations do not create independent configurations.
Rigid copies belong to an invariance test, not additional geometric-family coverage.
Show child-operation counts only as subordinate workload/performance information.

Always lead reports with independent cases planned, numerically evaluated, natively
attempted, built, and fully audited within the stated scope. Give both numerator
and denominator. If no native builds occurred, say "0 native attempts; native
failure rate unmeasured," never imply zero failures means geometric success.

3. Establish breadth before optimizing depth
-------------------------------------------
Separate development/tuning cases from locked validation cases before refinement.
Include straight/gentle controls and materially different bend, spatial, twist,
entry/exit and split-connection families, plus explicitly labeled invalid controls.
Choose useful feasible controls, not only fixed paths already rejected by policy.
Do not claim durability from a matrix consisting mostly of invalid inputs.

Reserve unseen configurations across relevant families. Once an outcome is used to
choose an algorithm or parameter policy, that case is development evidence; it is
no longer an untouched holdout. Repeated examination of validation results also
consumes the holdout. Keep identity/history rather than relabeling tuned cases.

Use the same algorithm, initialization policy, search budget, acceptance rules and
measurement policy across comparison cases. Historical hand-tuned seeds cannot
compete silently against cold starts. Report differing budgets as non-comparable.
Do not enter another single-case refinement batch until diverse-case evidence has
been reported and the user explicitly chooses that bounded diagnostic detour.

4. Keep four different outcomes separate
----------------------------------------
A. Numerical/preflight: no detected violation, known rule rejection, search budget
   exhausted, or harness error. "Unresolved" is not a predicted pass.
B. Native construction: not attempted, built, kernel failure, or interrupted.
C. Post-build audit: passed the named measured checks, failed, incomplete or absent.
D. Overall case: accepted only within the declared evidence scope, otherwise failed
   or unresolved. Neither visual appeal nor construction alone establishes compliance.

Retain all cases in the disposition table, including early exits, timeouts, skipped
builds and missing audits. For positive cases, exhaustion is an unsuccessful solver
outcome, not a success with an explanation. Expected-invalid controls get a separate
correct-rejection measure; do not inflate positive-case success with them. Report
classification accuracy separately from success at producing usable ribbons.

A failed conservative certificate is not proof that all possible geometry is
invalid. A finite unsuccessful search is not proof of infeasibility. Sampled checks
are not continuous guarantees. State unresolved clearance, strain, interpolation
and optimality properties explicitly. Never promise 100% accuracy from finite tests.

5. Test predictions against actual complete geometry
---------------------------------------------------
Register a native prediction before building; do not assign predictions afterward.
Cross-tabulate predicted build outcomes against observed outcomes, with unknowns
explicit. Separately compare predicted rule compliance against post-build audits.
A rule-rejected shape can still construct; that is not a kernel prediction error.
Investigate every false acceptance before claiming greater robustness.

Numerical screening can reduce cost but cannot substitute for native validation.
Preselect native samples from different families, including accepted, borderline
and rejected candidates where safe and explicitly diagnostic. Audit trunk, both
cap junctions, all relevant split endings and connection geometry. If construction
is blocked by a guard, report "not attempted" rather than "kernel failed."
Do not omit difficult outcomes from the native validation plan without disclosure.

7. Mandatory stop and review gates
----------------------------------
Stop scheduling more work when any approved budget is reached, a false acceptance
appears, a representative regression occurs, measured runtime exceeds the approved
estimate/cap, memory approaches its ceiling, or source/fixture hashes differ from
the plan. Also stop when continuing would require changing targets, rules, budgets,
audit coverage or replacing holdouts. Preserve available evidence and report the
specific issue; request direction before broadening the experiment or fixing it.

At each planned review, show independent-case outcomes and cumulative resource use,
including whether improvement transfers beyond the tuned specimen. A better number
on the same specimen is not sufficient grounds for another automatic iteration.
Do not interrupt an in-flight Fusion kernel operation by assuming client termination
cancels it. A stop gate normally prevents the next launch; escalate host recovery
separately if the process becomes unresponsive.

10. User-directed collateral regression protocol — 2026-10-05
------------------------------------------------------------
At a stopping point, preserve the triggering complete case and register three
structurally different complete-ribbon challenges to expose a proposed fix breaking
OTHER mechanics. These are not three solutions, retries, rigid copies or deliberate
reproductions of the same failure. Each has distinct fixed geometry/boundary
conditions and an explicit collateral-mechanics purpose. Retain existing controls.

Freeze all three inputs and establish their pre-fix baselines before evaluating an
adjustment. Apply one unchanged proposed fix and the same budgets/policies across
the triggering case, all three challenges and retained controls. Never adjust the
challenges to rescue the fix. A case already failing in its baseline is diagnostic;
it cannot establish a newly introduced regression or passing preservation by itself.
Compare new hard findings, native outcomes, mechanical goal values/warnings and
resource cost separately; do not hide regression behind aggregate success counts.

New fixtures do not authorize native builds, unlimited search or an automatic fix.
Obtain bounded resource scope as above. If native baselines are absent, say so;
numerical preservation alone does not prove construction preservation. These
examined challenges are development regression cases, not untouched holdouts.
