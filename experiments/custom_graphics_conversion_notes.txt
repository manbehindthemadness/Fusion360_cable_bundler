Custom Graphics conversion research — 2026-10-06

Question: can the triangle graphics used for non-finalized cable stripes become
persistent Fusion mesh bodies, BRep surfaces or smooth NURBS geometry?

Existing production architecture
------------------------------
cable_bundler/fusion/cable_solid_parts/stripes.py creates CustomGraphicsMesh
objects from StripeMeshResult.vertices and triangle_indices. The finalized path
already sends the same data to MeshBodies.addByTriangleMeshData. Both convert
millimeter input to Fusion's internal centimeters. No production change is needed
to establish the mesh-body route.

Documented routes
-----------------
CustomGraphicsMesh exposes coordinates and vertexIndexList for readback:
https://help.autodesk.com/cloudhelp/ENU/Fusion-360-API/files/fusion_CustomGraphicsMesh.htm
Persistent MeshBody can be created with addByTriangleMeshData:
https://help.autodesk.com/cloudhelp/ENU/Fusion-360-API/files/fusion_MeshBodies_addByTriangleMeshData.htm
MeshConvertFeatures, introduced July 2025, is currently marked PREVIEW:
https://help.autodesk.com/cloudhelp/ENU/Fusion-360-API/files/fusion_MeshConvertFeatures.htm
Faceted conversion makes individual BRep faces; prismatic conversion merges
inferred feature groups; organic conversion requires the Design Extension:
https://help.autodesk.com/cloudhelp/ENU/Fusion-360-API/files/fusion_MeshConvertMethodTypes.htm
An open mesh becomes a surface body; a watertight mesh becomes a solid:
https://help.autodesk.com/cloudhelp/ENU/Fusion-Mesh/files/MESH-CONVERT-TO-SOLID.htm
NurbsSurface.create constructs transient geometry from explicit control points,
degrees, knots and weights. It does not recover a smooth surface from triangles:
https://help.autodesk.com/cloudhelp/ENU/Fusion-360-API/files/core_NurbsSurface_create.htm
Inference: smooth stripe reconstruction needs a separate fitting procedure or
construction from the authoritative route/profile data. Faceted conversion alone
does not restore lost curvature or analytic surface definitions. Graphics/group/
component transforms must also be accounted for outside the identity test here.
